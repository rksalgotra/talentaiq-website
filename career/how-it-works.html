1 · Create your profile

Sign up and set your preferences: the kind of roles you want, cities you're open to, and your expected salary range. This gives the agent the boundaries it needs before it starts searching on your behalf — it only applies to roles inside what you've told it you want.

2 · Upload your resume

Upload once, in PDF or DOCX. The AI extracts your skills, work history, and education so it has a structured picture of your background — the same information a recruiter would look for, just parsed automatically.

3 · AI matches and tailors

The agent searches relevant listings and scores them against your profile. For each strong match, it rewrites your resume and drafts a short cover note specific to that job description — not a generic copy sent everywhere.

4 · Applies and notifies you

Tailored applications are submitted on your behalf. Every time something happens — an application goes out, a recruiter responds — you get a message on WhatsApp or email, so you're never left checking a dashboard to find out.
